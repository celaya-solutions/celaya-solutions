## Featured research: covering64

**The puzzle.** Sixteen people are split into teams of five. How few teams do you need so that every group of three people shares at least one team?

The best answer anyone has found uses **65 teams** (Rade Belić, 1997). Simple counting shows you need **at least 61**. The true number is unknown.

**What we found.** We proved that **61 teams cannot work**, which raises the known minimum to **62**. The proof is computer-assisted, and its proof files pass an independent checker.

→ **[covering64](https://github.com/celaya-solutions/covering64)** has every search, proof certificate and checker, so anyone can verify the work.
